📖 About StudentHub

StudentHub is a simple Student Management System developed to help students manage their academic activities. It provides easy access to courses, assignments, notes, profile details and important information.

🎯 Objectives

  • Manage student courses.
  • Submit assignments.
  • Download notes.
  • Update student profile.
  • View announcements.

⭐ Why Choose StudentHub?

StudentHub provides a simple and user-friendly platform where students can organize their academic work, save time and access important information from one place.
